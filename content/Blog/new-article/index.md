## The first answer is a beginning

Astronomy rewards patience. A point of light can be a planet, a distant star, an instrument artifact, or simply a question waiting for better data. The useful habit is not memorizing every possibility; it is learning to notice what would distinguish one explanation from another.

That habit travels well. In engineering, a circuit that behaves unexpectedly is not being difficult for its own sake. It is telling you that a model, a measurement, or an assumption needs another look.

I am trying to practice that second look more deliberately: slow down, write down what I actually observed, and let the next experiment be smaller and clearer than the last one.

## A place to return to

I wanted a place that could hold more than a list of links. A personal site is a small promise to pay attention: to write down what I am learning, to keep the useful parts of a project, and to make room for ideas that do not fit neatly into a classroom or a feed.

I am studying electrical engineering at Sharif University of Technology, with a background in astronomy and a continuing interest in mathematics. Those subjects have different vocabularies, but they share a habit I value: start with a question, make the assumptions visible, and keep looking when the first explanation is not enough.

This blog will grow slowly. I expect to write about engineering, astronomy, problem solving, books, and the practical details of building things. The goal is not to sound finished. It is to leave a clear trail of thought that I can follow later, and that might be useful to someone else too.

Thanks for stopping by.

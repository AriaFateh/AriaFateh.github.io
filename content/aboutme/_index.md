## Hello

I am Aria Fateh Kerdari, an electrical engineering student at Sharif University of Technology. I am drawn to problems that sit between precise theory and things you can actually build.

Before engineering became my main home, astronomy gave me a way to think about scale, evidence, and wonder. I was fortunate to earn a gold medal at the International Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics (IOAA), an experience that still shapes how I learn and collaborate.

## What I am exploring

- Electrical engineering and the mathematics behind useful systems
- Astronomy, physics, and the habit of asking better questions
- Programming as a tool for testing ideas and making them tangible
- Writing as a way to turn scattered notes into something shareable

This site is deliberately simple: a place for finished thoughts, unfinished questions, and the occasional record of how one idea became another.
